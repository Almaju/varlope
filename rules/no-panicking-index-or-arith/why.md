Slicing one string at another's length (`&keyword_lowercase[query_string.len()..]`) panics as soon as the second is longer; a Yelp suggestion change crashed Nightly that way and had to be backed out (#6712, #6717). Indexes, subtractions and `as` casts on sizes, counts and timestamps from outside need `.get()`, `checked_*`/`saturating_*` or `try_from`.
