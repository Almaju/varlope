Typos are the single most frequent review nit here (`identiifer`, `flexibilty`, `nothign`, `isntead`), and they cost a review round each time. In doc comments, UDL and the CHANGELOG they also ship: they end up in the generated Kotlin/Swift docs and the release notes, and a misspelled identifier becomes API. A dictionary of known misspellings finds them without guessing at words it doesn't know.
