A component reaches Kotlin and Swift only if it is wired into each megazord: a dependency plus a `pub use` in the megazord's `lib.rs` (without the re-export its FFI is not linked in), the right `uniffi.toml` settings, and on Android a Gradle module that is listed in `.buildconfig-android.yml`. Focus once lost `rust-log-forwarder` that way (#5858), and a missing `uniffi.toml` setting crashed Fenix (#5531); reviewers check the whole list by hand for every new crate.
