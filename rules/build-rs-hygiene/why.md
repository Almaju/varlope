A `build.rs` runs on every developer's machine and in mozilla-central's build: shelling out to `python3` or `bash` breaks on machines without them (Windows, a fresh Mac), writing into `src/` dirties the checkout and races parallel builds, and a bare `unwrap()` on a missing tool or variable leaves people with a panic and no next step. Reviewers asked for exactly this when Nimbus generated Glean code from its build script.
