Rust logs end up in app logs that users attach to bug reports, and `report_error!` messages and error strings go to Sentry. A history URL, page title, username or a whole sync record printed with `{:?}` leaks private data there. Reviewers check new log lines and error messages for URLs and origins (#7110) and replace records with guids (#5245); use a guid, a count or `redact_url(..)`.
