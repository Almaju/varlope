The CHANGELOG is what consumers read at each release, and every PR edits it, so bad merges and rebases land here first: entries added to (or deleted from) an already released version, a component's entries split across two `### Autofill` headings, a component written as a `##` section, stray blank lines. #7668 had to clean up exactly that for v159 and v160. Released sections are history; new entries go in the `(In progress)` section under their `### <Component>` heading.
