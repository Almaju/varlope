Reviewers spend comments on mechanical idioms a lint can catch: a hand-written `Default` that `#[derive(Default)]` would produce ("we really should have derived `Default` for that struct"), `pub fn new()` without `Default`, `struct Participation {}` instead of a unit struct, bare `Default::default()` where the type name reads better, and `format!("{}", x)` instead of `format!("{x}")`. A short, curated list keeps the signal without turning on all of `clippy::pedantic`.
