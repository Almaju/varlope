`INSERT OR REPLACE` deletes the conflicting row before inserting, so on a table that others reference with `ON DELETE CASCADE` it silently wipes their rows. `LIKE :q || '%'` treats a `%` or `_` typed by the user as a wildcard and cannot use an index, and `COUNT(*) > 0` reads every matching row where `EXISTS` stops at the first. Reviewers keep correcting these by hand in Suggest and Places queries.
