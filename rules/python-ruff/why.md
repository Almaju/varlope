CI scripts are Python, and a failure they swallow turns into a green task: `upload_symbols.py` dropped `main()`'s status so releases passed with no symbols uploaded (#6369), and `update-moz-central-vendoring.py` ignored `subprocess` failures (#6242). varlope checks those two patterns (`sys.exit(main())`, `subprocess.run` with `check=`) plus the ruff nits reviewers keep leaving by hand; the rest belongs to `ruff check` and `ruff format` in CI.
