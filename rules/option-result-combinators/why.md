The most frequent idiom nit in reviews: a hand-written `match` on an `Option` or `Result` where a combinator says the same thing in one line (`map_err`, `map_or`, `and_then`, `unwrap_or_default`) or `let … else` removes a level of nesting ("would feel more rustish to me"). The combinator names the intent, so readers do not have to check each arm.
