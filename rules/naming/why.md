Cryptic abbreviations were the most repeated nit in nimbus and nimbus-fml reviews ("`nm` is not at
all clear", "`ta` is not a readable variable name", "`og`?"): every reader has to reverse-engineer
what the author had in mind, and the same `vt` means "value type" in one function and "variables
type" in the next. New type names also follow Rust's convention of treating acronyms as words
(`JsonError`, not `JSONError`).
