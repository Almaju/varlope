A steady stream of review nits: `.clone()` on a value that is not used afterwards, `.to_string()` on what `format!` already returned, `"".to_string()` instead of `String::new()`. Each one is a hidden allocation, and noise that makes the clones that matter (an `Arc` handed to another thread, written `Arc::clone(&x)` so it reads as a cheap ref-count bump) harder to spot.
