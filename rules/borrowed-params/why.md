A `&Vec<T>`, `&String` or `&Option<T>` parameter forces every caller to own exactly that container, so tests end up writing `&"fi".to_string()` and callers build a `Vec` they did not need. `&[T]`, `&str` and `Option<&T>` accept all of those and more ("Most of the time when you have a `&Option<T>` you really want an `Option<&T>`"). UniFFI-exported signatures are left alone: the scaffolding hands over owned values anyway.
